This app helps you track menstrual-cycle symptoms day by day and evaluate
your records manually following the NER rules (Rötzer), in the spirit of
INER — but as an independent tool with no ties to INER: the app is not
reviewed, supported or endorsed by INER.

The evaluation stays with you: you place every mark on the cycle chart
yourself. The app only does the arithmetic you would otherwise do by hand
— the baseline drawn from the six prior low measurements, counts, lines,
and statistics (cycle and phase lengths). It computes and displays; it
never suggests and never decides ("assisted marking", Mode M). For a
reliable interpretation of fertility you need knowledge of the method —
from a textbook or a course.

What makes this app what it is:

• All your data stays on the device — no cloud, no analytics, no network
  permission.
• German interface by default, switchable to English.
• Daily entry of basal body temperature, bleeding (with exclusion flags
  for disturbances such as illness, alcohol or travel, and for spotting),
  cervical mucus in the NER shorthand notation (t, Ø, f, S), optional
  cervix, pain, mood, sex, and notes.
• Evaluation tools: first temperature above the baseline, count of low
  measurements, mucus peak day, calendar-based fertile window, exclusion
  of disturbed days.
• JSON export and JSON import in the settings — your data always remain
  your own.
• Open source; licensed under Apache-2.0.

Note: "NER Cycle App" is a working title; the final name has not been chosen
yet.
